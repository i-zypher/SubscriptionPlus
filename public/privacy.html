Privacy Policy

Subscription Plus · Effective November 13, 2026

The short version: Subscription Plus is a parody game. No real money is ever charged and you never create an account. We don't run ads, analytics or tracking, and we never sell or share your information. The one thing that leaves your device is what you type to Brad, our AI support agent, so he can reply. We don't store it.

Who we are

Subscription Plus is made by Ceymer Studios LLC, Jefferson Hills, Pennsylvania, USA ("we", "us"). This policy covers the game on the web at ceymer-studios.com/subscriptionplus (and subscriptionplusgame.com) and the Subscription Plus apps for Android and iOS.

Brad and our AI provider

Brad is an AI. When you chat with him, your messages and the recent conversation are sent to Anthropic, PBC, which runs the AI model that writes his replies. Anthropic automatically deletes these within 30 days. If its safety systems flag a conversation for breaking its usage policy, it may keep it for up to 2 years. You can read how Anthropic handles data at privacy.claude.com.

Please don't share personal information with Brad (your real name, address, phone number, passwords or anything sensitive). He doesn't need it, and he will absolutely try to sell you a plan based on it.

If Brad isn't available, the game uses "Brad Lite", a scripted version that runs entirely on your device and sends nothing anywhere.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or logins.
  • No real payments. Every "charge" in the game is fake, and the card number is made up.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies and no advertising IDs.
  • We don't sell or rent your information, or share it for advertising.
  • We don't use what you type to Brad to build a profile of you.

Links to other sites

The game links to the Subscription Plus album page (DistroKid) and lets you share your receipt using your phone's or browser's sharing tools. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Children

Subscription Plus isn't made for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has shared personal information with Brad, contact us and we'll help.

Where your information is processed

Our servers and providers are in the United States. If you play from elsewhere, what you type to Brad is processed in the US.

Your choices and rights

  • Don't use the chat with Brad if you'd rather nothing you type leaves your device. The rest of the game doesn't need it.
  • Turn reminders off anytime in Settings → Notifications, or in your phone's settings.
  • Clear your settings by clearing the app's or site's data, or by uninstalling.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information and to object to its use. We keep almost nothing, but if you have a request or a question, email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. Unlike your subscription, it won't renew without telling you.
